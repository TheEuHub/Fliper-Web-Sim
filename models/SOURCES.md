### Coordinate system and fidelity

The upstream bounds (mm) are X −33.594765…6.494766, Y −24.006536…76.294769, Z −23.000067…2.600000. The drawing gives nominal **100.3 × 40.1 × 25.6 mm**. To put the screen up and its text the right way round, the converter uses a rigid cyclic rotation and translation:

```
desk X = source Y − midY    (device length; display left to right)
desk Y = source Z − minZ    (height above desk)
desk Z = source X − midX    (device width; display top to bottom)
```

Result: **100.30131 × 40.08953 × 25.60007 mm** (length × width × thickness). No rescaling, smoothing, reconstruction, decimation or cap faces. Exact equal vertices are welded. The 21,693 positions are little-endian float32 and the 130,158 indices are uint16, embedded as base64. All **43,386** triangles survive, including winding; the offline verifier compares the *multiset of all faces* to the transformed source. Flat-shaded materials preserve facet edges.

### Part selection

Union-find finds 17 connected components. The buttons join the main shell (41,986 triangles); the other 16 components remain body material. On the main component, whole facets inside the measured front grooves are assigned to controls. At rest, their combined surface is exactly the input mesh. D-pad sectors pivot together, OK and Back slide independently. Existing moulded arrow/back marks receive dark ink on the white button material, not added geometry.

### Display and controls

The drawing and mesh put the rounded display recess at source X ≈ −28.5…−6.5, Y ≈ 1…38. The active area is a **36.1 × 18.05 mm (2:1)** rectangle inside that recess, centred at desk **(−6.644, 25.135, −3.950)**. Its 0.035 mm separation from the Z=2.10 face avoids z-fighting. LCD top points to −Z; the remaining rim is unlit bezel. Compare the [drawing](source/flipper-zero-drawing.png) to the [part inspection and asymmetric L/R checker](../docs/verification/parts-and-lcd.jpg).

The D-pad hinge is at (25.356, 25.100, −3.950); calibrated targets tilt the original disc by `atan(travel / lever length)`. Default travel is 0.55 mm for directions and 0.65 mm for OK/Back. The production manifest is in the generated mesh; `manifest.js` validates and applies optional local overrides. `?calibrate` has position/size/rotation, anchor/axis/travel, an LCD checker, factory restore and copyable JSON. Screen sizes and positions use mm; rotations use radians.

## Companion phone

Original procedural rounded bodies in [`../js/models.js`](../js/models.js), project MIT license. Standard: 68 × 146 × 8 mm nominal; compact: 60 × 128 × 8 mm. Both use rounded, UV-mapped displays and white side clickers with small stationary hit targets covering their travel. There is no third-party phone mesh or texture. No separately verifiable CC0 phone was selected from [ToxSam/open-source-3D-assets](https://github.com/ToxSam/open-source-3D-assets) or [devanshutak25/3d-resources](https://github.com/devanshutak25/3d-resources); neither collection is a license for an arbitrary model.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, run `python3 tools/convert-model.py`. No install required. `python3 tools/verify-offline.py` checks byte-for-byte reproducibility, source hashes, facets, script syntax, and test fixtures for ASCII STL and static GLB. Generic conversion: `python3 tools/convert-model.py --id my-static-mesh input.glb output.js`. The generic path bakes embedded GLB 2.0 scene transforms and converts metres to mm; named primitives are indexed. It is a **geometry converter**, not a runtime loader or an automatic screen/controls detector for arbitrary devices. Skins, morphs, compressed/sparse accessors and external resources are not supported.
